## 1. Overview

### 1.1 The game in one paragraph
- **Board:** 7 reels × 5 rows. Clusters of 5 or more matching symbols, connected horizontally or vertically, pay and then explode. The board tumbles until no win is left.
- **Board spots:** every explosion marks its **cell** in the board background.
  - The second explosion on a cell turns the mark into a ×2 spot.
  - Every later explosion on that cell doubles it, up to ×128.
  - A cluster's win is multiplied by the **sum** of the ×2-or-higher spots under it.
- **Multiplier symbols** (working name "multiplier coin", id `M`, values ×2 to ×100) can drop in every mode. When a tumble sequence ends with a win, that sequence's win is multiplied by the **sum** of the coins on the board.
- **Scatter** `S`, the "hidden bonus": 4 or more at the end of a tumble sequence trigger the Bonus with 10–20 free spins. Scatters that tumble in count. During free spins the spots persist for the whole feature.
- **Max win:** 20,000× the base bet in every mode.
- **Modes:**
  - Base, 1×.
  - Extra Chance, 1.5×. A persistent toggle.
  - Boost Spin, 100×. One spin with all 35 spots pre-seeded and a guaranteed win.
  - Bonus, 100×. A paying base spin with 4–7 S, then 10–20 free spins.
  - Super Bonus, 250×. Like Bonus, but every spot starts marked.
- **Style:** no characters and no mascot.

## 3. Board multiplier spots

### 3.1 The rule (math, S2)
1. **The grid.** `position_multipliers[reel][row]` is the unpadded 7×5 grid. Values:
   - **0** = no spot;
   - **1** = marked, with no multiplier and no number shown;
   - **2–128** = a multiplier.
2. **Order in each tumble step.** This is the standard order of the math-sdk cluster sample, and the Swamp Funk fixtures already use it (mock/games/swamp-funk/books/bonus_fixtures.json `tumble_chain`: `winInfo` → `updateTumbleWin` → `updateGrid` → `tumbleBoard`).
   - Cluster wins are evaluated against the grid **as it was before this step**, and `winInfo` is emitted.
   - Every exploding cell is then updated: 0→1, 1→2, and v≥2 → min(2v, 128). This is emitted as `updateGrid`.
   - Then `tumbleBoard`.
3. **Cluster win** = `pay(sym,size) × max(1, Σ spot values ≥2 under the cluster's cells)`. Marked cells (value 1) add nothing.
4. **Spots belong to cells, not to symbols.** A cell holding S or M cannot explode, so its spot does not change.
5. **Cap ×128 in every mode.** One cell needs 8 explosions to reach it: mark, ×2, ×4, ×8, ×16, ×32, ×64, ×128.

**Why spots are summed, not multiplied**
- The SDK sample sums (0_0_cluster/game_calculations.py:33-36).
- A product is uncontrollable: five cells at ×8 multiply to 32,768 but sum to 40. A sum is bounded at 35×128 = 4,480.
- Sums stay integers, so payouts stay on the 0.1× grid (§2.4).
- Sums read clearly as labels such as "×2+×4 = ×6".

**Why the cap is ×128**
- The reference shows 4/32/128.
- *(proto)* A cap of 64 moved the bonus mean by under 2%.
- The sample's 512 cap (0_0_cluster/game_config.py:83) only adds tail mass that the SDK volatility checks penalise (§10.3).

**Full math delta against the sample (v2).** The sample adds +1 per explosion and counts marked 1s (game_executables.py:16-29, game_calculations.py:33-36). Its base `run_spin` never touches the grid. Its free-spin loop emits `updateGrid` after `wincap` and has no wincap break (gamestate.py:8-58). The retrigger reads the trigger table unclamped (executables.py:80-84). The overrides below fix all of that:

# game_calculations.py: evaluate_clusters_with_grid ---------------------------
board_mult = max(1, sum(grid[r][c] for (r, c) in cluster if grid[r][c] >= 2))

# gamestate.py ----------------------------------------------------------------
def run_spin(self, sim, simulation_seed=None):
    self.reset_seed(sim); self.repeat = True
    while self.repeat:
        self.reset_book()                                    # tumble_win = 0; reset_grid_mults()
        self.draw_board(emit_event=False)                    # forced S per criterion (D20)
        if self.get_current_distribution_conditions().get("seed_values"):   # BOOST
            self.seed_grid_mults()                           # all 35 cells from seed_values
            update_grid_mult_event(self)                     # seed BEFORE the reveal
            reveal_event(self)
        else:
            reveal_event(self)
            update_grid_mult_event(self)                     # zeros AFTER the reveal
        self.tumble_sequence()
        self.set_end_tumble_event()
        self.win_manager.update_gametype_wins(self.gametype)
        if not self.wincap_triggered and self.check_fs_condition() and self.check_freespin_entry():
            self.run_freespin_from_base()                    # no free spins after a base cap
        self.evaluate_finalwin()
        self.check_repeat()
    self.imprint_wins()

def run_freespin(self):
    self.reset_fs_spin()
    while self.fs < self.tot_fs and not self.wincap_triggered:
        self.update_freespin()                               # updateFreeSpin
        self.draw_board()                                    # reveal (freegame)
        update_grid_mult_event(self)                         # persisted grid restated
        self.tumble_sequence()
        self.set_end_tumble_event()
        self.win_manager.update_gametype_wins(self.gametype)
        if not self.wincap_triggered and self.check_fs_condition():
            self.update_fs_retrigger_amt()                   # clamped at 7
    self.end_freespin()                                      # freeSpinEnd

# game_override.py ------------------------------------------------------------
def reset_fs_spin(self):
    super().reset_fs_spin()
    self.reset_grid_mults()
    if self.get_current_distribution_conditions().get("prefill_marked"):   # SUPER
        self.position_multipliers = [[1] * 5 for _ in range(7)]
        update_grid_mult_event(self)                         # right after freeSpinTrigger

### 4.1 Values and generation weights (S2)

### 4.2 When and how coins apply
- **When:** at the end of each spin's tumble sequence, if that spin won and at least one coin is on the final board. Coins that tumbled in count.
- **How:** `spinWin = ΣM × Σ_clusters pay × max(1, Σspots≥2)`. Spots act inside each cluster first, then coins act on the sequence total.
- **No win:** coins do nothing, no event is emitted, and the front end plays the coin `miss`.
- **No global multiplier** carries across free spins (D10).
- **Math:** `set_end_tumble_event` from the `0_0_scatter` pattern (§3.1), applied in **every** gametype, skipped after a cap, with `evaluate_wincap()` called after `set_spin_win`.
- **Effect *(proto)*:**
  - coins multiply about 12% of base spins, which is about 39% of winning base spins;
  - the free-spin mean is 38× without coins and 110× with them.
- **Not the SDK `symbol` multiplier strategy.** That strategy sums multipliers inside winning positions (math-sdk/gamestate/wins/+page.svx:43).

## 5. Scatter and free spins

### 5.1 Scatter ("hidden bonus")
- S lands anywhere, never forms clusters and never pays (D18).
- **Scatters that land during tumbles count**, both toward triggering and toward the award. The count is taken at the **end** of the tumble sequence.
- **Math (D20).**
  - The base `freegame` criterion forces 3–7 S on the reveal: `scatter_triggers {3:13, 4:75, 5:10, 6:1.6, 7:0.4}` *(proto start; the optimiser reweights)*.
  - A 3-S book where no 4th S tumbles in fails `check_repeat` (force_freegame without a trigger) and is redrawn, about 25 attempts per kept book *(proto)*.
  - Non-freegame criteria keep the SDK behaviour: reveals with 4+ S are redrawn (board.py:206-209), and a tumble-completed trigger is discarded (executables.py:51-58).
  - The rarer 2-S-plus-two-tumble-ins path (≈5% of natural triggers) is not forced in v1 (§12). The rule still holds; that path is just not sampled.
- "Hidden" is marketing only. Every trigger path is disclosed (checklist 29).

### 5.2 Awards
| Scatters (end of sequence) | Base game | During free spins (retrigger) |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | – | +5 |
| 4 | Bonus, 10 FS | +10 |
| 5 | Bonus, 12 FS | +12 |
| 6 | Bonus, 15 FS | +15 |
| 7+ | Bonus, 20 FS | +20 |

- **7+ clamp** in both `update_freespin_amount` and `update_fs_retrigger_amt`, because tumbles can bring 8 or more S (executables.py:72-84).
- **No free spins after a cap:** a base sequence that reaches the cap never enters free spins (§3.1 guard). No retrigger is checked after a cap.
- **Natural free spins:** ≈1 in 260–290 base spins at ≈100× *(proto 1 in 261 at 100k, 1 in 271–287 at 400k)*. About 18% are completed by tumbles.
- **Anticipation:**
  - The SDK default is min−1, so 3 S on the reveal (about 1 in 50 spins).
  - Set `anticipation_triggers[freegame]=3`, because the default would tease at 2 S in about 6% of free spins.
  - The front end shows it only from `reveal.anticipation[reel] > 0` (Board.ts:339-366). There is **no tumble-drop anticipation in v1**.
- `fs_trigger_event` mutates `special_syms_on_board` rows in place (math-sdk events.py:62). Do not reuse those positions afterwards.

### 5.3 Bonus (natural or bought)
- **Bought (`BONUS`, 100×), D22.** The round opens with one **basegame** spin whose reveal is forced to 4–7 S (`scatter_triggers {4:80,5:15,6:4,7:1}`).
  - That spin pays normally (clusters, spots, coins at base values), and S that tumble in can raise the award.
  - Then 10–20 free spins follow.
- **Spots:** the bought spin's spots clear when free spins start. Free spins start at all 0 and keep their spots for the whole feature (§3.3).
- **Coins:** free-spin values, applied per spin (§4).
- **Bet:** free spins play at the base bet of the triggering round.
- **Feature total:** `freeSpinEnd.amount` counts free-spin wins only. Its `winLevel` uses the endFeature table 1/5/10/20/50/100/500/2000/cap. The round total adds the opening spin.

### 5.4 Super Bonus (`SUPER`, 250×)
- Bought only (D7). Same as the Bonus, including the opening paying spin, except that when free spins start **every spot is marked (1)**. The first explosion on any cell then gives ×2.
- **Presentation:**
  - the `fs:trigger` intro;
  - then the animated E3 clear of the opening spin's spots;
  - then the prefill `updateGrid(all 1)` plays as a 35-cell ignite wave (E16, not deferred);
  - then spin 1.
  - The Super state uses a distinct frame and background grade. It is game state, restored on resume.
- *(proto)* Mean 274–278× against 115–116× for the bonus. Those prototypes leave out the opening spin (§10.1).

### 5.5 Engine flow (exists)
- Core `fs:trigger {retrigger}` and `fs:end`, then the switch back to base (handlers.ts:211-248), plus the `fs:update` counter.
- The mode lives in `RoundPlayback.betMode` (handlers.ts:52-53), but scene modules cannot see it. **E11** adds `mode` to `round:start` (events.ts:104; emitted at controller.ts:402, 489, 694, 918).

### 5.6 Resume of a free-spin round (S3 §4.2)
Restore all of this before the recorded free spin replays:
1. The bet and mode, from `round.amount` / `round.mode` (controller.ts:467-473). **EXTRA stays selected after the round (D23).** Today `endRoundCleanup` resets `activeMode` (controller.ts:586). E10 keeps `baseMode` instead.
2. The spot grid from the end of the previous free spin, Super prefill included (§3.6).
3. Coin values (E5).
4. The free-spin counter (current/total), the round total, and the Bonus or Super visual state. Core `fs:update`, `win:total` and `mode:change` restore the first three. The Super state is game state in `restoreGameScene`.
5. No feature intro. Show "Resuming your unfinished round…" (src/flow/messages.ts:20), then the spin.

`BONUS`/`SUPER` use `auto_close_disabled=True`, so a zero-total bonus can still be resumed. `endRoundPolicy` keys on `round.active` (src/flow/endRound.ts:18-21).

## 7. Book events and payloads

### 7.1 Units
- **Amounts** are integers: bet multiple × 100, always in **base-bet** units, even in 100× modes. Every amount is a multiple of 10 (§2.4).
- **Padded rows** (visible rows 1–5): `positions`, `explodingSymbols`, `overlay`, `multInfo.positions`.
- **Unpadded:** `gridMultipliers` is `[reel][0..4]`.
- **Multiplier values** are plain integers.
- **Win levels** are in base-bet units. The front end applies E20 for tiers.
- `payoutMultiplier` (= `finalWin`) is relative to the base amount, not the debited total (docs/STAKE_ENGINE.md:72-78).

### 7.2 Symbols in boards (`json_ready_sym`, math-sdk events.py:7-18)
- `{"name":"H1"}` for a paying symbol;
- `{"name":"S","scatter":true}` for a scatter;
- `{"name":"M","multiplier":25}` for a coin. Padding and refill coins carry the value too.

### 7.3 Round skeletons
```
BASE / EXTRA : reveal · updateGrid(zeros) · [winInfo · updateTumbleWin · updateGrid · tumbleBoard]*
               · [boardMultiplierInfo · updateTumbleWin]? · setWin? · setTotalWin
               · freeSpinTrigger? → per spin: updateFreeSpin · reveal(freegame) · updateGrid(persisted)
                 · [steps]* · [coins]? · setWin? · setTotalWin · freeSpinRetrigger?
               · freeSpinEnd (feature only) · finalWin
               (a natural trigger may come from a 3-S reveal plus a tumbled-in S)
BONUS        : same; the opening basegame reveal has 4–7 forced S and pays normally
SUPER        : same as BONUS; updateGrid(all 1s) right after freeSpinTrigger (before the first updateFreeSpin)
BOOST        : updateGrid(seeded) · reveal · [steps]+ · [coins]? · setWin · setTotalWin · finalWin
wincap       : … winInfo · updateTumbleWin · wincap · setTotalWin · [freeSpinEnd] · finalWin
coin wincap  : … boardMultiplierInfo · updateTumbleWin · wincap · setTotalWin · [freeSpinEnd] · finalWin
base wincap  : no freeSpinTrigger after it, even with 4+ S on the board
```

### 7.6 Wincap (implement explicitly)
- `evaluate_wincap` (math-sdk executables.py:35-41) sets `wincap_triggered` and emits `wincap {amount: 2000000}`. It is called inside `emit_tumble_win_events` and after the coin step.
- **After it:**
  - `update_grid_mults` returns early, so there is no `updateGrid`;
  - the tumble loop stops, so there is no `tumbleBoard`;
  - the coin step is skipped;
  - `setWin` is suppressed;
  - `setTotalWin 2000000` is emitted.
- The free-spin loop is `while self.fs < self.tot_fs and not self.wincap_triggered`; the sample has no break (0_0_cluster/gamestate.py:37).
- No retrigger is checked after the cap. **A base-game cap never enters free spins** (`run_spin` guard). If a forced-freegame book caps in its base spin, `check_repeat` redraws it.
- A feature round closes with `freeSpinEnd`, then `finalWin 2000000`.
- **Cap reached by coins:** `boardMultiplierInfo → updateTumbleWin → wincap`.
- The SDK does not truncate the step. Each emitted amount is limited to the cap, but the internal running total may exceed it (`update_final_win`, state.py:190-211). The front end counts to `wincap.amount`.

- **New:** a `boardMultiplierInfo` game handler that emits `mult:collect`, and its `foldGameEvent` (no state beyond E5 values).

**What the validator checks**
- finalWin = the last setTotalWin = payoutMultiplier. Every amount is a multiple of 10. `winInfo.totalWin` = Σ win.
- clusterMult = the ≥2 spot sum from the grid **before** the following `updateGrid`.
- Each in-step `updateGrid` changes exactly the exploding cells, along 0→1→2→4…→128.
- boardMult = Σ coins on the final board, and totalWin = tumbleWin × boardMult.
- **After `wincap`, only `setTotalWin`, `freeSpinEnd` and `finalWin` appear.** No `updateGrid`, `tumbleBoard`, `boardMultiplierInfo`, `setWin` or `freeSpinTrigger`/`Retrigger`.
- The first BASE/EXTRA `updateGrid` is all zeros.
- **BOOST:**
  - the first event is `updateGrid` with every value in {2,4,8,16}, and it precedes `reveal`;
  - no S;
  - non-zero final.
- **SUPER:** an all-1 `updateGrid` immediately follows `freeSpinTrigger`.
- Free-spin awards follow §5.2, clamped at 7.
- Every M object, padding and refills included, carries a `multiplier` from the published list.

## 12. Open decisions for the user

1. **Extra Chance price.**
   - 1.5× (default): ≈×2.2 bonus chance, passes the SDK limits.
   - The reference 3×: ≈×5.8 (1 in ~45), etl40b ≈1.9 against 0.9, confirm mandatory.
   - 2× sits on the edge (≈1.1).
2. **A second special spin** (≤500×, one-shot, richer seeds). It must be decided before submission, because modes cannot be added after approval.
