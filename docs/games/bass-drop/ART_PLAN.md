## 1. The look

**A neon bayou juke joint at party volume, in a premium hand-painted finish.** It is the Swamp Funk world (same room, symbol set, key-colour rules, hex HUD), rebuilt around sound:

- the reels sit between a **speaker stack** on the left, whose huge woofer *is* the Groove Meter, and **Baron Croak's DJ booth** on the right;
- two brass **horn speakers** are bolted on the top corners of the cypress frame and pump on every boom;
- **Gumbo** (heavyset adult alligator bouncer: maroon tank top, jeans, rubber boots, gold tooth, toothpick) leans on the lower cabinet; **Baron Croak** (lanky adult bullfrog DJ: backwards cap, hot-pink bowling shirt, headphones around the neck, gold chain with a record pendant) slams the drop button;
- feature emblems: a **gold-and-teal jukebox** (Juke Jam) and a **crowned hot-pink speaker stack** (Mega Mix);
- everything that booms is a speaker: dark plum-black wood cabinets with polished gold bolts, charcoal cones, dark glass dust caps.

**Finish** ([STYLE_DECISION.md](STYLE_DECISION.md), stored as `artbible.bassDrop.styleFormula` / `environmentFormula` and passed to every Bass Drop prompt as `--var STYLE_FORMULA=…`; the Swamp Funk formula stays untouched so every recorded job keeps reproducing):

| | Bass Drop (formula D) | What changed from the Swamp Funk locks (ART_BIBLE §3) |
|---|---|---|
| Outline | Clean, confident, **single-weight bold black** outline around every shape; thinner dark interior lines | Same colour; mascots 6–8 units and symbols ≈ 3% of the cell still hold |
| Rendering | Rich painterly shading with **soft gradients**, jewel-tone colours, glossy lacquered highlights, crisp white specular hotspots | Replaces "1 base + 2 hard shadow tones, no gradients" |
| Light | Key light **upper left** + warm bounce + a **thin cool rim light** | The foreground rim light is now allowed (thin, cool); key direction unchanged |
| Depth | Clear three-dimensional volume from the rendering | **No plum extrusion.** Never write "extrusion" (it draws a stick) or "molten" (it draws drips): say "polished solid gold". Checked on every planned prompt |
| Glow | **Never painted.** Meter rim, button glow, LEDs, wild trail and title shine are runtime `fx_*` slots | Unchanged |
| Backgrounds | Painterly soft brushwork, atmospheric perspective, violet-indigo and teal night palette with amber and magenta neon; calm dark centre 60%; brightest neon at the far left and right | Replaces the flat-cel plate |
| Camera | Symbols frontal three-quarter, slight low angle; mascots three-quarter toward the reels (Gumbo faces screen-right, Croak screen-left); meter and cabinets orthographic front (the woofer is a true circle). **Exception:** the DJ booth is seen slightly from above so the platter reads | Unchanged |
| Canvas | Symbols on the 360×360 @2x canvas; every other rig at 2× its landscape design size (ANIMATION_SET §0); never upscale | Unchanged |

**No words in art.** Titles, counts, WILD, ×N, prices and the game name are live text on `txt_*` slots or a typeset vector word-mark. The logo emblem is generated with a **blank banner**; a typographer sets SWAMP FUNK / BASS DROP on it. Game and feature titles are banned from prompts (the model would paint them).

**Hue watch-outs** (ART_BIBLE §4): H2 pink and W teal equal the background neon hues and must read as enamel, not glow; the meter trim band is generated light grey and recoloured per mode in code (teal / gold / pink); the multiplier badge is generated once in gold (t3) and hue-mapped to t1 teal, t2 lime, t4 orange, t5 pink (DESIGN §9).

**Key colours.** The requested hex goes into the prompt, but the model does not always obey it (one D symbol came back on olive). Every matte step therefore runs `tools/matte/outline_matte.py --key auto` (border median, implemented) and checks the border is uniform and near the requested hex (`keyUniform` gate), else regenerates. **`keyUniform` is not automated yet** (tools/matte has no such check): until it is, the reviewer checks the four corners by eye, and the matte's own halo gate catches a wrong key.

| Assets | Requested key | Why |
|---|---|---|
| H1–H4, meter, cabinets, horns, booth, W pieces, mega speaker emblem | `#00FF00` | no green in them |
| W (master, rig, parts), jukebox emblem (+ cracked), frame pieces, Gumbo | `#FF00FF` | teal or green content |
| Croak, notch icons, logo emblem | `#0000FF` | green + pink, or teal + pink, clash with both |

### How a row runs

1. **Render** (script-free): the row's `render` field, e.g. `python3 tools/gen/genlib.py render --template 'prop.txt#A' --var 'PROP=…' --var 'VIEW=…' --var 'DETAIL=…' --var 'STYLE_FORMULA=…' --var 'KEY_HEX=#00FF00' --json`. It prints the prompt and its sha256 (`promptHash`); the renderer fails on an unfilled placeholder or a forbidden word. The Node twin (`tools/gen/lib/genlib.mjs`) prints the same bytes (tested).
2. **Approve references** in [`art/plan/approvals.json`](../../../art/plan/approvals.json): the chosen candidate's job id per row.
3. **Batch spec:** `python3 art/plan/build_plan.py spec --batch c01 > art/_work/hf-plans/bd_c01.spec.json`, then `pnpm gen:hf-ingest plan --spec art/_work/hf-plans/bd_c01.spec.json` prints the exact `generate_image_batch` call. Each candidate is its own job (`<row>.c1`, `<row>.c2`); references are passed as **Higgsfield job ids** (`medias: [{value: <job_id>, role: "image_references"}]`), so nothing local is uploaded.
4. **Generate** with the Higgsfield MCP, **always passing `model` explicitly**: the MCP's default image model is `gpt_image_2_5`, which is denylisted.
5. **Record + ingest** (`tools/gen/hf-ingest.mjs`, the ingestion track): `pnpm gen:hf-ingest record --plan … --from <MCP JSON>`, then `pnpm gen:hf-ingest` downloads into `art/_raw/<row id>/vNN/` and appends manifest rows.
6. **Review** against §6, add the pick to `approvals.json`, run the row's `downstream` steps (matte → cut/register → Spine), then the next batch. `spec` checks every pick is a completed ledger job of that row (exit 3 otherwise).
7. **Retry** a rejected row (the 1.5× allowance in §4): `python3 art/plan/build_plan.py spec --batch c01 --attempt 2 --rows sym_H2 --candidates 1` makes batch `bd_c01_r2` with job `sym_H2.r2.c1` (same prompt, same references); a batch id already in the ledger is refused, so a spec is never paid twice by mistake.

**Model choice.** Nano Banana Pro everywhere: the adopted anchors were made with it, and NB2 costs the same at 2k. The three H3 eye edits use **NB2 at 1k** (1.5 instead of 2 credits): each edits the whole approved rig-ready master (referenced by job id, nothing uploaded), and at 1k the crawfish is still ~870 px tall, above the ~600 px its cell content needs at 2x, so the eye region is never upscaled. The hidden-area **fills** also use NB2, because it is the one model here with masked inpaint (`is_inpaint` + a `mask` media). Seedream and every other model are not used. **Never** any OpenAI model (`gpt_image_2`, `gpt_image_2_5`, `openai_hazel`): OpenAI's policy bars real-money gambling ([denylist](../../../licenses/denylist.json) `openai-gpt-image`). Higgsfield has no seed and no negative prompt, so consistency comes from the frozen formula plus the references.

**Resolution.** 2k for anything that ends ≤ ~1,300 units at the 2× authoring size (symbols, props, emblems, cards, rig masters, hand and prop sheets). 4k for background plates, the Croak design sheet, and the mascot **body** and **face** sheets, whose pieces must reach the 2× authoring size after cutting (Croak is 1,260 units tall; a torso piece on a 2k sheet would need upscaling, which the bible forbids).

**Candidates.** 2 where a row sets the look of what follows and has no approved image of its own to copy (the new H2/H4 beauties, the W pieces, the meter, both emblems, the mascot rig masters and body sheets); 1 for derived rows (rig-ready masters, parts sheets, edits, icons, the Croak D sheet), which follow an approved reference.

**References** (≤ 4 per row, identity first). Style anchors: `sym_H1` (gold, speaker cones), `sym_W` (teal enamel), `sym_H3` (character-like symbol), all formula D; `sym_H2` for anything black and glossy once approved. Backgrounds use the painted plate. Mascots use their D design sheet for identity and, for the part sheets, the approved rig master for view and scale. Parts sheets and edits use the approved master as the **only** reference. Nothing from `probe1` (formula A) is referenced.

## 6. Review rubric per asset

Gates are the [ART_BIBLE §10](../../ART_BIBLE.md#10-qa-gates) set (ids from `artbible.gates`; the JSON lists them per row) plus `keyUniform` (the measured key background is uniform and near the requested hex). Automated today: halo and canvas/pivot (the matte, exit 1), and for rigs the budgets, adult proportions, clip lengths and events (`tools/spine/validate.mjs`). Palette ΔE, outline histogram, silhouette confusion, style similarity, OCR no-text, darker centre and `keyUniform` are **planned** (PIPELINE 2.1), so the reviewer applies them by eye until they exist; then the art director. **Retry budget:** 2 regenerations per asset, then the best result goes to a human (paintover if needed). The palette-ΔE, outline-histogram and style-similarity thresholds were set for the flat-cel finish: **recalibrate them on the approved formula-D anchors** before they may fail a row (soft gradients add colour clusters; compare base tones only).

**Every foreground asset (formula D):** a single-weight black outline with no sketchy double line; no drips on gold, no stick or tab poking out of the object; the rim light thin and cool, never a glow; nothing glowing, no light rays, no cast shadow; no text or pseudo-letters.

## 7. Licence, provenance and the Higgsfield clearance caveat

- **Build and preview only.** `licenses/allowlist.json` → `higgsfield` has `clearance: pending`. Higgsfield outputs, including the adopted anchors, may be generated, matted, rigged and previewed, but **nothing made from them may ship** until the written clearance is filed in `licenses/clearances/` (training opt-out, real-money gambling use incl. EU/UK, output ownership and confidentiality, pass-through of Google's terms: [STACK § Image route](../../STACK.md#image-route-higgsfield-or-vertex--scenario-you-decide)).
- **The audit enforces it on `public/assets` today, not only at release.** `tools/licence/audit.mjs` fails on any file in `public/assets` whose manifest chain includes a licence that is not `cleared`, and on any shipped vendor generation with `tosVersion: null` (there is no `licenses/tos/higgsfield/` archive yet). Previews must load from `build/` or `art/source/`, never from `public/assets`, until then.
- **Manifest rows** (written by `pnpm gen:hf-ingest`): `route: "higgsfield-mcp"`, `vendor: "Higgsfield"`, `model` = the id the job reports (`nano_banana_2` for Nano Banana Pro), `seed: null`, `jobId`, `promptPath`, `promptHash`, `refHashes`, `licenseId: "higgsfield"`, `shipped: false`.
- **Model-id collision to watch:** a Nano Banana Pro job *reports* `nano_banana_2`, which is also the MCP id of Nano Banana 2. The requested MCP id is kept in the ledger (`batch.model` / `request_model`); read it there, not from the row's `model`, to know which model made an NB2 row.
- **No local uploads** in this plan: every reference is a Higgsfield job id, so no unreleased art leaves the repo beyond what the three rounds already sent.
- **SynthID** is in every Nano Banana image and cannot be removed; decide a disclosure policy with counsel (ART_BIBLE §11). The paintover log and `humanEditor` / `humanEditSummary` fields are the authorship record.
- If the clearance does not come, the same rows run on **Vertex Nano Banana Pro** (`tools/gen/nbp.py`) with the same templates and hashes; only the references must be uploaded as files instead of job ids.

## 8. Open decisions for the art director

1. **Static board sprite vs the Spine rig.** Symbols show a static sprite and swap to a pooled Spine instance for actions (ANIMATION_SET §2.7). If the static sprite is the beauty master and the rig is built from the rig-ready master, the swap can pop. Recommendation: the board sprite is the matte of the approved **rig-ready** master (or the rig's setup pose rendered); the beauty master serves the paytable, card art and marketing.
2. **Royals under formula D.** Flat vector royals will look cheap next to painterly symbols. Options: gradient shading built into the vector (free, planned), or an AI material pass, which needs a formula-D version of `royal_material_pass.txt` (the current one says "extrusion" and "hard-edged cel"; it is shared with Swamp Funk).
3. **Gumbo's chain and Croak's cap.** The adopted Gumbo D design has no gold chain (ANIMATION_SET §5.1 has a `chain` slot and chain physics); Croak v2 wears a backwards cap (no `cap` slot in §5.2) and headphones around the neck (the cups parent to the neck). The part lists follow the adopted designs; ANIMATION_SET needs the matching edits.
4. **Light note on H1 and H3.** The adopted beauties were rendered without the bible's "slightly more from above" note for tilted symbols; the new rig-ready masters and H4 keep it. Check the pairs side by side; drop the note for them if it shows.
5. **Right horn:** mirrored (free; its key light reads upper right) or the P2 light-correct row.
6. **Card art:** generated illustrations (P1, 6 credits) or composites of approved pieces (free, flatter).
7. **Portrait plate:** reframe edit + width crop (planned, no outpaint credits) vs outpaint.
8. **Frame:** the Swamp Funk cypress design in formula D (planned) or a Bass Drop variant with speaker-bolt corners.
9. **Booth camera exception** (seen slightly from above).
10. **Tablet (1920×1920) plate.** No row generates one: the tablet layout is the landscape composition moved down 420 px (DESIGN §15.3).
