# Style: the reference draw style, extracted (no theme, no mascot)

Measured from the three Sugar Gates reference screenshots (board, intro cards, buy menu). This is the **vibe** to match: drawing rules, value structure and composition. Theme, objects and names are ours. `doNotCopy` lists what must differ.

## Style formula (symbols, frame, UI art)

Bright, premium casual-mobile slot-game art. A uniform, thick, near-black warm-brown outline around every shape, with thinner dark interior lines. Smooth soft-gradient shading in two or three steps, no texture noise, no visible brush strokes. Highly saturated clean hues, one dominant colour per object, with a deeper shade of that colour along the shadow-side rim inside the outline. Key light from the upper left: one large soft-edged white highlight plus a small crisp white hotspot on every curved surface, and a gentle core shadow toward the lower right. Chunky, rounded, simplified volumes; bold silhouettes that read instantly at small size; glossy lacquered enamel finish.

## Environment formula (background)

Hand-painted casual slot-game environment shown as a soft-focus backdrop: shallow depth of field, every shape blurred and simplified, no outlines, gentle painterly gradients. High-key hazy daylight with warm cream highlights and cool pastel shadows; the theme's hues shifted to light pastel tints, far less saturated than the foreground. Large rounded props framing the bottom-left and bottom-right corners, mid-ground scenery masses at both sides, a floor plane at the bottom, a plain low-detail centre, tiny floating sparkle motes.

## Board composition (measured)

Measured on the 1077x607 (16:9) board screenshot. (1) PLACEMENT: the board is centred horizontally (well centre x=537.5 = screen centre) and fills the full height above the HUD: frame crown top at about 1-3% from the top, base bar resting directly on the HUD band. The total frame including its corner flourishes spans about 64% of the screen width (x 195-880); the symbol well alone spans 44.5% of the width (x 298-777) and 67% of the height (y 103-510). Cell pitch is about 80x81 px, square, about 13.4% of the screen height. (2) FRAME STRUCTURE (top-heavy, slim sides, thin bottom): a crown or header about 14-16% of the screen height above the well, carrying the most ornament and a centred gold ornament; slim side posts about 4.5% of the screen width each, sitting flush against the well; gold flourishes flaring outward about 5% beyond the posts at all four corners (top and bottom); a thin gold sill bar about 3.6% of the height under the well. It is a two-material frame: a light neutral body (ivory) plus saturated cartoon gold trim (lemon-yellow base, orange shadow bands, cream-white highlight streaks, rendered in flat-ish bands, not realistic metal). The frame has the same thick near-black warm-brown outline as the symbols and is the sharpest and brightest object on screen (edge energy about 1.6x the symbols, about 30x the background). (3) WELL: near-black blue-slate (#13161F to #222939), split into vertical column lanes by thin darker gaps; each lane has a faint top-to-bottom gradient. The dark well is the one dark mass on a bright screen, so the saturated symbols pop through value inversion. (4) BACKGROUND AROUND IT: visible as about an 18% strip at the left, about 18% at the right and a thin strip above the crown. It is high-key (V 0.74-0.90), low saturation (S about 0.30) and heavily defocused (Laplacian edge energy about 1/20 of the board), with no dark vignette at the screen edges. Large soft props sit in the bottom-left and bottom-right corners and are partly overlapped by the frame flourishes; sparkle motes float on top. (5) LOGO ZONE: the right-hand margin, upper third (about x 81-93%, y 15-42%), a stacked two-line wordmark about 12% of the screen wide. In the reference a mascot head peeks above it; we have none, so the logo stands alone or sits on a small non-character emblem. The left margin is pure background. (6) HUD BAND: a solid full-width band along the bottom 12.4% of the height (y 532-607), dark warm taupe #4C4634, separated from the gold sill by a dark line. The user's own UI goes here; the board must leave it clear. (7) 7x5 ADAPTATION: at the same cell height, 7 lanes give a 1.4:1 well about 52-53% of the screen wide; posts plus flourishes bring the frame to about 67-70%, leaving about 15-16% margins (the logo still fits right, upper third). For the 1920x1080 master: cell about 140 px, well about 980x700, crown about 150 px, sill about 38 px, HUD band about 134 px. The frame must be built as 3- or 9-slice pieces (crown, post, sill, corner flourish) so it stretches from 6 to 7 columns and across layouts.

## Symbol tier rules

Size, framing and colour make three clearly separate reading layers. PREMIUM (4 in the reference): an iconic object on a rounded-square TILE that fills about 90% of the cell (measured 74x72 px of an 80x81 cell). Neighbouring tiles almost touch, so premium clusters read as a paved block. Each tile has a bevelled bronze-copper border (more orange than the frame gold) with ornamental corner brackets, and an inner field in one saturated colour with a lighter radial centre: one field colour per premium (red, royal blue, leaf green, teal). The object fills about 85% of the tile and slightly breaks the top edge (a leaf or rim overlapping the border) for depth. Lesson from the reference: the object hue should be complementary to its field (yellow on red reads best; green on teal reads worst). LOW (5 in the reference): FRAMELESS glossy single objects sitting directly on the dark well, at only about 70% of the cell (56x55 px), so dark space surrounds them. One dominant hue each (pink, yellow, blue, pink, purple), at very high value (V 0.92-0.97) and saturation (S 0.72-0.80). Each has a big soft white highlight at about 10 o'clock, a small hotspot, a deeper same-hue rim band on the shadow side, and a thick dark outline. Reference weakness to avoid: two lows (the swirl lollipops) differ only in colour, and pink appears on two lows. Ours must differ in silhouette AND hue (passing the 64 px greyscale silhouette gate). MULTIPLIER SYMBOL: a round coin at about 93% of the cell, slightly bigger than the tiles and much bigger than the lows. It has a thick patterned gold rim and a saturated green inset that no other symbol uses. Round versus square versus small floating is the instant tier cue. SPECIAL / SCATTER ("hidden bonus"): not visible on the board screenshot. The intro and menu art use an oversized cluster object and a winged gold medallion, so the scatter should be frameless, oversized (about 102-115% of the cell, breaking the cell bounds) and carry its own glow aura, with a silhouette distinct from the tiles, coins and lows. BOARD SPOT TILES (background multipliers): flat, matte, rounded-square colour fills the size of the cell, drawn UNDER the symbol layer, with no bevel and no gloss, so they never compete with the glossy symbols. The tier is read from colour alone (reference ramp: sky-blue low, coral-pink mid, lime-yellow high). The value is shown in chunky outlined numerals tinted a stronger shade of the tile hue. Rendering rule: everything on the symbol layer is glossy and outlined; everything on the board-background layer (spots, lanes) is flat and matte.

## Multiplier symbol and spot-tile look

MULTIPLIER SYMBOLS (dropping coins): a round gold medallion about 93% of the cell, with a thick bevelled gold rim carrying a repeating border pattern, a saturated green faceted inset, and a faint darker emblem embossed in the inset behind the number. The VALUE is the dominant element. It uses chunky, rounded, heavy sans numerals plus a lowercase "x", spanning about 55-65% of the coin width for two digits, with a vertical gold-to-orange gradient fill, a thick dark-brown outline, a small lower-right drop-shadow step and a white glint on the top edge. The numerals sit dead centre and do not tilt. Values seen: 5x, 8x, 50x, 100x. The green inset is the only green-on-gold on the board, so coins are spotted instantly even in a crowded grid. For us, Higgsfield generates the blank medallion only (no numbers, per the no-baked-text rule). The value is a runtime BitmapText styled to match (gradient fill, heavy outline, step shadow), and the value tier can swap the inset colour or rim richness (bronze, gold, gem). BOARD SPOT MULTIPLIERS (from intro card 1): flat pastel-saturated rounded-square tiles, one per cell, colour-ramped by tier: blue at 4x, coral-pink at 32x, lime-yellow at 128x (so the ladder doubles: 2, 4, 8, ..., 128). Each carries a big outlined numeral in a stronger tint of the tile hue (cyan on blue, magenta on pink, yellow on lime) with a dark outline and a soft drop shadow, and the tiles are drawn slightly tilted on the card. On the live board no spot is active in the screenshot, so the in-play look is inferred: a cell-filling colour fill under the symbol, with the numeral showing when the cell is empty or during tumbles. Recommendation: render the spot fill and frame in code or as a 9-slice (flat, matte, no gloss), with live numerals. Map the ramp onto the existing swamp-funk SpotGrid heat bands (SPOT_BANDS_DOUBLING) using our own theme-derived colours, not the exact blue, pink and lime sequence.

## Do not copy

- The 'gates' frame: white/ivory marble fluted Greek columns flanking the board, the curved arched entablature crown with a centred gold acanthus scroll, and gold wing-like volutes flaring from the column capitals and bases (Sugar Gates and Gates of Olympus trade dress). Our frame may keep the top-heavy, slim-sides proportion but must use a different construction and material from our own theme.
- Greek key / meander patterns anywhere: premium tile corner brackets, multiplier coin rim, intro-card corner caps.
- The multiplier coin design: a gold coin with a green faceted gem centre and an embossed lightning bolt. Our multiplier needs a different object, shape language and inset colour.
- Any god, Zeus-like bearded elder, mascot or character (the user wants none; Stake also prohibits child-appealing characters).
- The Olympus premium set: lightning bolt, amphora or urn, grapes, laurel wreath (also crowns, chalices, rings and helmets as an obvious Olympus kit).
- The candy low set and theme: round hard candies, banded candy balls, jelly beans, swirl lollipops with striped sticks, soft-serve swirls, donuts, dripping icing, sprinkles.
- The pastel candy-palace background: pink and cream arched colonnade, balustraded stairs, icing drips on the cornices, giant soft-serve props in the corners.
- Logo trade dress: a stacked two-word wordmark with a pink rounded upper word over a gold slab lower word, the mascot peeking over the logo, the names 'Sugar', 'Gates', 'Sugar Gates' and the 'Colorful Play' brand mark.
- Feature names and art from the menu: 'Sweet Spin', 'Candy Spin', 'Super Bonus' with a winged gold X medallion, lollipop-bundle bonus art, and lightning-bolt volatility pips as a branded motif.
- Intro feature-card design: ivory marble-veined portrait cards with chamfered corners and gold triangular corner wedges.
- The exact spot-tile colour ladder (sky-blue 4x, then coral-pink 32x, then lime-yellow 128x) as a direct palette copy. Derive our own tier ramp from the new theme.
- The HUD look: a dark olive-taupe band with a gold-framed 'BONUS' plaque and a black circular spin button (the user has their own UI anyway).
- Do not upload the reference screenshots as Higgsfield image references: NBP copies columns, candies and coin designs. Transfer the style only through the written formula and our own approved anchors.

## Other observations

- VALUE STRUCTURE (the core of the vibe, measured): the screen has four planes. (a) Background: bright pastel and blurred (HSV S 0.29-0.35, V 0.74-0.90, Laplacian edge energy 1.8). (b) Frame: bright ivory and gold, the sharpest object (edge energy 55). (c) Well: near-black blue-slate #13161F to #222939 (V 0.12-0.16). (d) Symbols: highly saturated and bright (S 0.72-0.82, V 0.71-0.97, edge energy 34). Focus comes from value inversion (a dark well inside a bright world) plus sharpness and saturation contrast, not from a dark vignette.
- TEMPLATE OVERRIDE NEEDED: art/bible/prompts/background.txt section A says 'darker, lower-detail area in the central 60%' and 'brightest neon masses at the far left and far right'. That is wrong for this vibe. Use: 'calm, plain, low-detail centre at the same light value (the board covers it); largest rounded props in the bottom-left and bottom-right corners; no dark vignette, no neon'. The darkness comes from the board well, not the plate.
- BLUR STRATEGY: the reference background is heavily defocused (about 1/20 of the board's edge energy). Recommend generating the plate only moderately soft ('soft-focus', still with readable shapes) and applying a runtime blur of about 6-10 px at 1080p. The background can then react per state (spin: slightly more blur and a slow drift; connect: a warm light bloom pulse in the props; explode: a sparkle-mote burst and a brief exposure lift; feature: a variant plate crossfade with reduced blur). Generating it fully blurred locks out those reactions and makes image_decompose layering poor.
- OUTLINE: about 2-3 px at an 80 px cell (about 3% of the object width; about 9-11 px on the 360 px @2x symbol canvas). Its colour is a near-black warm brown or maroon (#1E0500 to #2A0D06), not pure #000. The same outline goes on frame, tiles, coins and lows, which binds everything into one style. The Higgsfield playbook rule applies: always name the outline explicitly, or batches drift.
- SPECULAR SIGNATURE: every glossy object has one large soft-edged white highlight blob at about 10-11 o'clock covering about 15-20% of the visible face, plus a tiny crisp hotspot, and a deeper same-hue rim band on the lower-right shadow side. Shading is smooth airbrushed gradients in 2-3 steps with no texture: simpler and cleaner than Bass Drop formula D (no painterly brushwork, no rim light, no bounce light). Do not reuse formula D verbatim for this game.
- FILL RATIOS for cellFill (measured content bbox / cell pitch): premium tiles about 0.90; multiplier coins about 0.93 (up to 0.98 including numerals); lows about 0.70 (stick objects up to 0.91 tall); scatter recommended 1.02-1.15. These differ from the swamp-funk bible values (royal 85-90%), so lows must be set deliberately smaller to get the paved-premium versus floating-low contrast.
- PROMPT WORDS TO AVOID in this game's briefs: 'extrusion' and 'molten' (STYLE_DECISION.md pitfalls; say 'polished solid gold'); every subject word from the reference (candy, sugar, sweet, lollipop, jelly, icing, marble column, Greek, Olympus, gates, wings, lightning); child-appeal words (cute, kawaii, chibi, toy, plush, baby, for kids); '3D render' or 'plastic' (pushes it to CGI vinyl and loses the outline); 'sticker' (adds white die-cut borders). Use 'glossy lacquered enamel finish' instead of toy or candy wording.
- PREMIUM TILES are a theme-neutral device worth keeping: a bevelled metal border plus a saturated coloured field per symbol, with the object breaking the top edge. Change the border shape language (for example a different corner treatment, rivets, gem studs or organic edges from our theme) and the field-colour set. Pick each field as the complement of its object's hue.
- LOW SYMBOLS: keep them frameless, glossy, single-hue and about 70% of the cell, but choose five objects with five clearly different silhouettes (for example sphere, teardrop, long oval, star or faceted shape, crescent or ring) and five different hues. The reference fails this (two swirl lollipops; pink used twice).
- PALETTE BEHAVIOUR: the foreground uses clean, fully saturated primaries and secondaries (hot pink, lemon yellow, cobalt blue, violet, leaf green) on the dark well. The background repeats the same hues as light pastel tints (pink, peach, cream, lilac, mint) in warm hazy light, so the world feels unified while the foreground pops. Any new theme should define its five or six foreground hues first, then derive the background from their pastel tints.
- MOTION AND FX cues in the reference stills: tiny white four-point twinkles and soft bokeh motes float over the background; nothing else moves in the stills. Spin, land, miss, connect and explode states will need our own FX. The look should stay in the same flat, glossy, outlined language: outlined burst stars, soft white flashes and chunky outlined debris, never realistic smoke or fire.
- INTRO and MENU screens (images 1 and 3): the intro shows the blurred plate alone with three portrait feature cards, a logo top-centre and live text with dark outlines. The menu thumbnails use saturated gradient backdrops (hot pink, cyan) scattered with outlined multiplier numerals in the same numeral style. These are UI-side and the user has their own menus, but a feature card or thumbnail art should follow the same formula with numerals as live text.
- 7x5 versus the reference 6x5: the reference well is 6 columns, so do not trace its frame width. Plan the 3- or 9-slice frame pieces so the crown's centre ornament stays centred while only plain segments stretch. Check that the logo still fits in the about 15% right margin at 16:9, and plan a portrait layout with the logo above the board.
